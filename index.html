About Me

Network & security track, hands-on since day one.

9Certifications earned in 2026

I'm an Information Technology graduate from the University of the Cordilleras, specialising in networking and security. My degree gave me the theory — the real learning came from supporting live networks: a regional government office in La Trinidad, and a school in Unzad.

At DOST-CAR I diagnosed dead zones across the building, helped redesign the LAN topology, installed Starlink as satellite backhaul and terminated the cabling that carries it. At Unzad Elementary School I enforce content safety and network access policy every week. Alongside that I build web software — a cafeteria POS with analytics as my capstone, and an intern attendance system as project lead.
